GEO & LLMs

The AI Citation Campaign

The Goal: Ensure the brand was consistently cited in generative AI answers (Claude, ChatGPT, Perplexity) for commercial HR queries.

The Execution: Pioneered a Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) outreach strategy. I secured brand mentions on specific high-authority hub pages and statistical roundups that LLMs index heavily for their training and RAG data.

The Result: Successfully established brand presence in AI-generated answers for key bottom-of-funnel queries, future-proofing visibility beyond traditional SERPs.

Turning a 'No' Into a Placement

The Pivot: Instead of pushing, I audited their highest-traffic posts and found a critical data gap in a piece from 2024. I offered to supply a custom graphic and proprietary 2026 data from factoHR to update their post—requiring zero writing on their end.

The Result: They accepted the asset, credited us with a do-follow link in their top-performing article, and requested future data collaborations. Value always wins.

My Psychology of Successful Campaigns

High reply rates are not luck. They are the output of a repeatable system that I adapt based on what the campaign actually needs, whether that's surgical precision or efficient scale.

1

Keyword & Competitor Intel

Before prospecting starts, I map every linkable asset and identify who is already linking to competing content. This tells me which doors are open before I knock, and which angles are most likely to land.

Prospecting That Fits the Brief

For targeted campaigns, strict DR, traffic, and topical relevancy filters narrow the list to sites that will actually move the needle. For high-volume campaigns, I use automation tools to build and segment prospect lists quickly, without compromising on relevancy. No PBNs. No link farms.

3

Personalization at the Right Level

For specific, high-value targets, I dig into the site and write openers that feel genuinely tailored. For scale campaigns, I use AI-assisted tools to build templates that read as personal without requiring manual effort on every send. The approach matches the output required.

Sequences that go out on time and follow-ups that add something new to the thread rather than just nudging. The goal is replies, not blocklists.
